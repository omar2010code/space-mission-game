How to play

  1. Pick a mission, then a science goal. Every NASA mission starts with a science question, and the goal decides which instruments you must carry.
  2. Drag parts from the left onto the rocket. A glowing line shows where a part will snap on: above, below or between parts. The spacecraft is three rooms: the Control room, the Communication room and Life support & shelter. Side parts (boosters, solar panels, instruments) snap onto the side of a room or a fuel tank.
  3. Mirror places side boosters and solar panels in pairs. Click a part to select it: fuel tanks can be made longer or shorter. Drag a part off the rocket to remove it.
  4. A decoupler starts a new stage. Everything below it burns first and is dropped when empty, so the rest flies lighter. Side boosters fire with their stage.
  5. Watch the gauges: cost, Δv (how much speed your fuel can give), thrust ÷ weight, electricity and air drag.
  6. A mission has four segments: launch (your rocket), space (the spacecraft), ground (stations that talk to it) and users (scientists). Pick ground and users on the right.
  7. Your design climbs Concept Maturity Levels from CML 1 to CML 4. At CML 4 the rocket takes its streamlined launch shape: the rooms become a winged spacecraft on a big fuel tank with boosters on the sides. Before launch, the Mission Design Lab grades it. Finishing Orbit unlocks the Moon.
