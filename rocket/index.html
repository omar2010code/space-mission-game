How to play

  1. Pick a mission. Each one has a budget and its own needs.
  2. Click parts on the left to add them. Your spacecraft is built from the top down: the first part is the nose.
  3. Click a part on the rocket to select it, then move it up or down or remove it.
  4. Put a decoupler between sections. Everything below a decoupler is one stage and is dropped when its fuel is used, so the rest flies lighter.
  5. Watch the gauges: cost, mass, electricity, lift-off thrust and Δv (how much speed change your fuel can give).
  6. Press Launch. The mission report tells you which step worked or failed, and why.
