How to play

  1. Play: the story. Build a spacecraft that passes the test, launch it, and deal with what happens on the way. Your place in the story is saved.
  2. Chapter 1: the rocket workshop. Pick a mission, then build a rocket from the nose down. Every part costs money, adds mass and uses or makes electricity. Watch the gauges and press Launch. The mission report tells you which step failed, and why.
  3. Chapter 2: adrift in orbit. Once your mission succeeds, board the spacecraft. Push off with WASD or the arrow keys and float through the hatches. Press M for the ship map.
  4. Chapter 3: moon rescue. Drive the buggy along the marked road, follow the lost rover's wheel tracks, and bring its sample back to the lander before the oxygen runs low.
  5. New to rockets? Start with the Tutorial in the workshop.
